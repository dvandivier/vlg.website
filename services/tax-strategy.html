Where owners usually leave money on the table

These are the areas we look at first. Not every one fits every business, and that's what the review is for.

01

Payroll taxes

If you have W-2 employees, payroll tax is one of your biggest recurring costs. ProfitGuard Plus is built to lower it while raising your team's take-home pay.

02

Entity structure

Sole prop, LLC, S-Corp, or C-Corp. The setup that made sense when you started may not fit the profit you're making now.

03

How you pay yourself

The split between a reasonable salary and distributions can change what you owe in self-employment and payroll tax.

04

Retirement plans for owners

SIMPLE IRAs, Solo 401(k)s, and defined benefit or cash balance plans can let higher-earning owners set aside more before tax.

05

Deductions most owners miss

Hiring family members for real work, renting your home to your business for meetings, and accountable plans for business expenses.

06

Succession and exit

What happens to the business, and the people who depend on it, is part of the plan. See our estate planning page.

Common questions

I already have a CPA. Why would I need this?

Most CPAs are busy preparing returns for what already happened. Tax strategy is about planning before the year ends. We work alongside your CPA, and your CPA still files.

Are these strategies aggressive?

No. Everything we look at is written into the tax code and has been for years. The key is setting it up correctly and documenting it, which is exactly what we help with.

How do I know if it's worth my time?

If your business is profitable, you have W-2 employees, or you haven't looked at your entity setup in a few years, a review is usually worth it. If it isn't a fit, we'll tell you.

Do you prepare or file my taxes?

No. We design the strategy and coordinate with your CPA or tax preparer, who handles filing.

What does the first review cost?

Nothing. The first strategy call is free, and there's no obligation to move forward.
