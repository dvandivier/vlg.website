For owners with W-2 employees

ProfitGuard Plus

A benefits structure that lowers your payroll taxes and puts more money in your employees' pockets. Nobody's gross pay changes.

  • Roughly $500 to $600 per employee, per year, in employer payroll tax savings
  • About $40 to $80 more per paycheck for each participating employee
  • Preventative care benefits for your team at no cost to them
  • Your payroll provider keeps running payroll

For profitable business owners

Business Tax Strategy

Your tax bill is a planning decision, not a fixed cost. We look at how your business is set up and how you pay yourself, then build a plan for the year ahead.

  • Entity review: does your setup still fit your profit?
  • Owner pay: salary and distributions, structured on purpose
  • Retirement plans built for owners
  • Planned before year end, filed by your CPA
