VLG In The Community

Devin Vandivier spent his career serving as a law enforcement officer, EMT, and firefighter. VLG carries the same mission: show up, serve first, and help our neighbors. Legacy isn't built behind a desk. It's built in builder associations, chambers, schools, and senior communities across Central Texas.

Where you'll find us

Organizations we belong to and communities we serve.

  • Temple Area Builders Association (TABA) Member — serving the builders and trades that build Central Texas
  • Temple Chamber of Commerce Member — connected to the local business community
  • Morada Temple Senior living community — active independent living and skilled nursing — education events
  • Mariposa Apartment Homes Seminar circuit — on-site financial education workshops
  • Country Lane Senior Community Seminar circuit — on-site financial education workshops

How we give back

Community growth comes first. Here is where we put our time.

Nonprofit partnerships

We work alongside local nonprofits to support their causes and help them take care of the staff and volunteers who carry the mission.

Schools and municipalities

School districts, cities, and public employers serve all of us. We help them find ways to add value for the people who keep them running.

Financial education

Free workshops for employees, families, and seniors. Plain-English lessons on money, taxes, and planning that most of us were never taught.

Education events, open to everyone

VLG runs free workshops for business owners and families: cutting payroll and business taxes, planning your estate, and protecting what you have built. No products pushed. No hard sells. If you'd like VLG to speak at your nonprofit, school, association, or workplace, or you have a community cause we can help with, reach out.
